about

Built for transparent data retrieval.

This static site retrieves records, aggregates them where the data supports it, and keeps source details visible without requiring a backend.

In plain terms

  • The site loads prepared source files from data/prepared/ and checks selected public data services from the browser.
  • EM-DAT is used as prepared historical disaster data, with aggregates and row-level recent records where available.
  • World Bank Indicators are retrieved as country time series of actual returned values.
  • UNHCR and GDACS records are shown as returned by their public services when available.
  • This site retrieves and organises available records from selected public and prepared sources.
