Humanitarian Futures Simulator

Pick a country and a year. The simulator pulls public country-level time-series data, extends usable trends into your chosen year, and builds a transparent case file. It also adds supporting humanitarian and Earth-observation context where the site can retrieve it. It is not an official forecast.

Important: the simulator only projects indicators that have usable historical numeric data. It does not predict floods, wars, earthquakes, displacement shocks or humanitarian need. Dataset and event listings are included to show what contextual information exists, not to score severity.

Select a country and target year. One projection approach is used by default to keep the output understandable.

Recommended trend continuation
Uses the most recent usable historical data. Population and mortality use compound/log trends; percentages are capped between 0 and 100; impossible negative outputs are prevented.

Sources used or referenced by the simulator

Some sources provide numeric time series that can be projected. Others provide current events, dataset catalogues, or contextual metadata. They are deliberately kept separate.
