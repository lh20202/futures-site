Humanitarian futures data playground

Country-year case builder

Pick a country and target year. The site extends historical public-data trends where that is mathematically possible, then builds a concrete case file from the projected indicators, current disaster signals, humanitarian dataset discovery, Earth-observation catalogue results, and technology trend signals.

Important: projections here are simple mathematical continuations of past returned data, not forecasts. Disaster, conflict, displacement and technology disruption are not predicted as events. They are used as current/contextual signals or dataset-discovery evidence.

Uses browser-safe public APIs and catalogue searches. No hidden credentials.
